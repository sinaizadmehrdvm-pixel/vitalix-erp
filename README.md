VITALIX ERP
Intelligent Business Management & Enterprise Resource Planning Platform
VITALIX ERP is a modular business management platform designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises streamline financial and operational workflows.
Our Vision
To make integrated business management software more accessible, adaptable, and efficient for growing businesses, with an initial international expansion focus on Qatar and the GCC region.
Core Product Areas
- Accounting and financial management
- Sales and purchasing
- Inventory and warehouse management
- Customer relationship management (CRM)
- Order processing and distribution workflows
- Business reporting and operational insights
Product Development
VITALIX ERP is under active development, with an existing software MVP and ongoing improvements to its functionality, usability, and deployment processes.

The development roadmap includes:
- Multilingual capabilities and regional localization
- AI-assisted business analytics
- Workflow automation
- Advanced reporting and business intelligence
- Integration with third-party business services

Business Model
VITALIX ERP is being prepared for a B2B SaaS subscription model, with planned packages for SMEs and growing enterprises.

Market Expansion
Qatar is a priority market for future localization, pilot deployments, partnerships, and commercial expansion across the GCC.

Current Status
Development release: VITALIX ERP v1.4.1
Stage: MVP / Pre-commercialization
Commercial availability: Under preparation
